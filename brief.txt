- Repo name:         crypto-lab-matsui-line
- Short name (H1):   Matsui Line
- Subtitle:          Linear cryptanalysis · piling-up lemma · Matsui 1993
- One-liner:         Linear cryptanalysis where statistical bias in linear approximations of a cipher's S-boxes accumulates across rounds to recover key bits — the pair-mate attack to differential cryptanalysis.
- Concept to teach:  Every S-box has linear approximations that hold with probability ≠ ½. Individually the bias is tiny. But the piling-up lemma shows biases compound across rounds, and with enough known plaintext-ciphertext pairs, the accumulated bias becomes exploitable. The key recovery works by guessing key bits and checking which guess maximizes the bias — the correct guess wins.
- Primitives/spec:   Matsui 1993 ("Linear Cryptanalysis Method for DES Cipher"), Matsui 1994 (first experimental break of DES), piling-up lemma
- Accent (--accent): #6366f1
- Favicon emoji:     📐
- In scope:          Same toy SPN used by Biham Lens (consistency); linear approximation table (LAT) in place of the DDT — show the bias of every input/output mask pair for the S-box; piling-up lemma visualization: watch individual biases compound across 2, 3, 4 rounds; key recovery attack: generate known plaintext-ciphertext pairs, guess partial key bits, measure bias for each guess, the correct key produces the highest bias; "how many pairs do I need?" calculator based on accumulated bias; side-by-side with Biham Lens: differential uses chosen plaintext + difference propagation, linear uses known plaintext + bias accumulation; link to Biham Lens
- Non-goals:         No full DES linear cryptanalysis (too large for browser); no multidimensional linear cryptanalysis; no zero-correlation attacks; the toy SPN is for teaching, not a production cipher
